# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience is blog readers: people arriving via search, RSS, or social links to read posts on Rust, Go, Python, and JavaScript, or to browse Josh's writing more broadly. Prospective consulting clients (via `/consult`) are a secondary audience who land here to evaluate Josh's credibility before booking a call.

## Product Purpose

Josh Finnie's personal homepage and blog: a place to publish technical writing (Rust, Go, Python, JS, weird lit, leftist reading), share a professional history and resume, and offer software consulting services (fixed-fee website builds/migrations, AI pipeline audits, zero-to-one product engineering).

## Positioning

Seniority and breadth: 15+ years of full-stack experience spanning zero-to-one founding engineering work through staff/senior engineering leadership at larger orgs (People Data Labs, IndigoAg, PBS, TrackMaven). This range, not a narrow tech specialty or a single differentiator like AI tooling, is what a similar consultant or blogger could not truthfully claim.

## Operating Context

Content is authored as file-based MDX/Markdown in `src/content/` (Astro content collections) and published as a static Astro site to Netlify. Blog readers arrive cold via search/RSS/social; consulting prospects arrive from the `/consult` page and convert via Cal.com booking links (build, migration, AI audit, zero-to-one, general inquiry).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Blog with tags, series, and RSS feed
- About/resume page with work history, education, PGP key
- Projects, books, talks, "weird-lit" and "leftist" reading pages
- Consulting page (`/consult`) pitching four service lines: Website in a Box, WordPress → Jamstack migration, AI Slop Fixer, Zero-to-One Product Engineering
- No consulting client testimonials, logos, or case studies exist yet; none should be fabricated

## Brand Commitments

- Voice is personal and first-person ("I") for individual experience; Josh also writes some blog posts in first-person plural ("we") for collective/shared-discovery framing, per existing per-post convention
- No fabricated proof: never invent testimonials, client names, benchmarks, or case studies for the consulting page or elsewhere
- Personal, indie voice throughout, including the consulting page; not corporate-agency tone

## Evidence on Hand

- Real work history and education (About page): People Data Labs, IndigoAg, PBS, TrackMaven, Koansys; UMGC, CCSU, UConn
- Real consulting service lines and Cal.com booking links (Consult page)
- No client testimonials, logos, or case studies for consulting exist yet

## Product Principles

- Blog readers are the primary audience; consulting is secondary and must not crowd out the reading experience
- Let seniority and breadth carry the credibility argument, not manufactured social proof
- Preserve the personal, first-person voice everywhere, including commercial pages
- Never fabricate proof (testimonials, logos, case studies) for consulting or any other section

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility requirement has been established beyond standard web accessibility practice.
